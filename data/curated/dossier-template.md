# Award research dossier — copy for a specific year

Record concrete findings before moving this template into dossiers/{year}.md.

- Official award summary and scientific background URLs; date accessed.
- Every recipient ID, prize share, official motivation and distinct contribution.
- Candidate works: title, all authors, year, document type, DOI/ISBN; exact award association evidence.
- Primary text consulted: edition/version, provider/URL, relevant sections/pages; inaccessible works explicitly identified.
- Claims to verify: historical context, intuition, equations and symbol definitions, empirical findings, influence and caveats.
- Rights per version: external access; hosting/conversion evidence; default denial when uncertain.
- Portrait permissions: photographer, source, license, credit, modifications.
- Editorial checklist: citations, math, each recipient’s contribution, rights, exports, reviewer and date.
- Open questions / next source to read.
